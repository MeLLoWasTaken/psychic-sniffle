| 2026-10-03 | Duels are balanced through dampening, and every spec, tanks and healers included, is held to 40-60% in 1v1 (the human chose option A of review 6). Consequence: a 1v1 pickup at 1:30 gives mana but no health. The existing duel auras (healer, Carnage Warblade, Rime Arcanist; F-08) stay for now | The human: dampening "leaves each class's kits untouched and allows every specialization to engage with every aspect of the game". Ramping from zero cannot touch shields put up in the first seconds, hence the starting level and shields counting as healing. Local duels: the Warblade went from 0 of 4 to 3 of 4 against Radiance. Dampening cannot flip a tank: the Vanguard won on its larger health pool (72,000), and with 60,000 health the duels split 2-2, so equal health in duels is put to the human as a separate rule | Human |
